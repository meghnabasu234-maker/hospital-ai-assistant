APPOINTMENTS

Patients can book appointments with doctors according to their
availability. Patients should provide their patient ID, preferred
doctor, date, and time when requesting an appointment.

GENERAL INFORMATION

Patients should arrive at least 15 minutes before their scheduled
appointment. Emergency cases should be directed to the emergency
department immediately.
